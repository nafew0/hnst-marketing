# M5: Design apps (owner: Claude, Wave C, about 6 days)

> Paste `docs/RULES.md`, then the phone rules block in `docs/phases/PHONE.md` § 3, then this file, as the first message in the coder's session. Before coding, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` § Satus adaptations (§ E5 covers the phone layout) and `docs/phases/PHONE.md`.

**Depends on:** M2. Wall data comes from MSW until M4 merges.

**Build order:**

1. **Packages, App Store style** (`/packages`, `/packages/[tier]`).
   - A feature card for the Founder Package.
   - A list of the four packages, each with a price button. Prices come from the offers API; if it fails, the button reads "See price".
   - **A detail page for each package:**
     - a row of "screenshots" of the deliverables, drawn in code;
     - "What's included";
     - an Information list: price, turnaround, revisions and the tool-cost disclosures.
   - The price button offers "Book a call about this", which opens Calendar with the package chosen, or "I'm interested", which shows Astra's package lead form in a sheet.
   - No ratings or reviews. Reuse the package reveal where it fits.
2. **How it works:** a Settings-style grouped list. Each step opens its detail.
3. **About:** a contact-card profile of HONEST and the Founder, from the CMS about content. Nothing about anyone is invented.
4. **The HONEST Marketer:** a Books-style shelf. The cover is a stand-in until the real one exists. "Get the book" opens Astra's magnet form in a sheet.
5. **Photos,** with four albums:
   - **Proof:** the proof gallery data, with its labels kept.
   - **Wall:** visitors' photos from `GET /api/v1/wall`, labelled "Visitors' photos. Not clients or endorsements.", each with Report.
   - **Worlds:** the `public/worlds` loops, poster first.
   - **Your shots:** the memory store that M7 fills.

   The viewer swipes between photos and also works with buttons and a keyboard.

6. **Classic website** (the reference calls it Safari).
   - Opening it turns on the visit's classic-view flag.
   - A slim address bar shows the page's URL, and a "Home screen" pill returns to the iPhone layout.
   - The pages themselves are today's mobile pages, unchanged.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Each app works at three widths in WebKit and Chromium emulation.
- Content URLs still load complete pages with JavaScript off. Their metadata, canonical and JSON-LD are unchanged.
- Package prices match `/packages` exactly, and no ratings appear.
- Proof labels are intact.
- The Wall album shows approved photos only.
- Axe finds no serious or critical issues, and reduced motion works.
- Screenshots are attached to the PR.
