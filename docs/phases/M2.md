# M2: Phone shell and notifications (owner: Claude, Wave B, about 5 days)

> Paste `docs/RULES.md`, then the phone rules block in `docs/phases/PHONE.md` § 3, then this file, as the first message in the coder's session. Before coding, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` § Satus adaptations (§ E5 covers the phone layout) and `docs/phases/PHONE.md`.

**Depends on:** M1.

**Build order:**

1. **Icons.** Our own vector icons for every app: gold on glass, rounded squares. Extend `components/ui/window/dock-icons.tsx`, so desktop and phone share the art. The social marks follow each network's brand rules.
2. **Home screen.**
   - The wallpaper is a brand-world loop, poster first; tier 0 gets the poster only.
   - The widget row: **Next free call** reads the slots API and opens Calendar. **Add to Home Screen** is described in step 7.
   - The 12-icon grid is a labelled navigation list.
   - The dock has four icons.
   - Social icons come from the CMS `socialLinks` and are hidden when empty.
3. **Lock screen.**
   - Date and clock in Bodoni Moda, with the same wallpaper.
   - The notification stack.
   - Unlock with a tap or a swipe up.
   - Shown on the first visit of a session only, and skipped when the visit starts at an app's URL.
4. **The app frame** (the phone skin of the P7W window).
   - An iPhone top bar with a back chevron and a centred title, and a home bar at the bottom.
   - Going home keeps the app warm in memory (the P7W store), so drafts survive.
   - The zoom out of the icon and back into it uses the measuring in `genie.ts`.
   - Back, Esc, `document.title` and focus behave as in P7W.
   - Phone-only apps open through `?app=`. Direct visits and no-JS behave as today.
5. **Primitives for app builders**, documented in `components/ui/phone/README.md` and shown at `/lab/phone` (noindex):
   - `PhoneApp`, the nav bar and a large title;
   - a grouped list and its rows;
   - a bottom sheet and a segmented control;
   - buttons, and empty, loading and error states.
   - **`PhoneView`**: how a content app's page shows its phone view. The page's normal content stays `desktop-only`, and the app's view is `mobile-only`, in both the intercepted route and the direct page. Settle in M2 whether that duplicated HTML is acceptable, and on which pages.
6. **Notifications** through the `notify(trigger, vars)` API, in three places:
   - **Lock-screen cards.**
   - **Banners:** they slide from the top and stay about 5 seconds. A focus or a finger on one pauses it; a swipe up or × dismisses it. No more than three appear per visit, each at most once, and never while the visitor is typing or using the camera.
   - **Notification Centre:** pull down from the top of the home screen, or tap the date. It has a Clear button.

   Banners are announced in a polite live region and never take focus. Copy comes from M3's fields, with in-code fallbacks marked DRAFT until those land.

7. **Add to Home Screen.** Android uses its install prompt. iPhones get a sheet showing Share → Add to Home Screen. The widget is hidden when the site is already running from the home screen.
8. **The HONEST app** reveals the homepage story, which is already in the HTML under the home screen.
9. **Edges:** safe-area insets, `100dvh`, swipes kept away from the edges, and the Android back button.
10. **Follow-ups from the M1 spike** (see the M1 PR's report):
    - On a direct visit, the home indicator overlaps the page's last lines and the "Privacy choices" pill. Give it a backdrop, and keep the pill clear.
    - The desktop dock's About icon is a compass, which reads as Apple's Safari on a phone. Redraw it.
    - Icon gradients appear only after hydration, because their defs are client-only for the calendar's date. Make the defs server-safe.
    - Rewrite `e2e/windows.e2e.ts`'s phone cases for the phone layout (the macOS dock isn't on phones there).
    - Decide on `viewport-fit=cover` for the safe areas, which emulation can't show.
    - A window's scroll pane is `tabindex="-1"` (P7W). When a page has nothing focusable and scrolls, axe flags it (`scrollable-region-focusable`); seen in WebKit with an empty local CMS. Make the pane reachable by keyboard.

**Acceptance criteria**

- **Phone sizes:** at 375, 390 and 430 px wide, lock screen → home → each placeholder app → home works in WebKit and Chromium mobile emulation.
- **Desktop:** at 800 px and wider, screenshots of `/` and an open window match `main`, and `windows.e2e.ts` passes.
- **No-JS:** at phone size, `/` shows the homepage story, and app URLs show complete pages.
- **Accessibility:** axe finds no serious or critical issues on the lock screen, the home screen, an app or the Notification Centre. A keyboard reaches every icon, and every gesture has a button.
- **Notifications:** e2e covers the limit of three, once per visit, and nothing while typing.
- **Reduced motion and tier 0:** no video, zoom or slide, and everything still works.
- **Screenshots** of every state are attached to the PR.
