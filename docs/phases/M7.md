# M7: Camera and the wall (owner: Claude, Wave D, about 3 days)

> Paste `docs/RULES.md`, then the phone rules block in `docs/phases/PHONE.md` § 3, then this file, as the first message in the coder's session. Before coding, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` § Satus adaptations (§ E5 covers the phone layout) and `docs/phases/PHONE.md`.

**Depends on:** M5 (Photos). It builds against MSW, then checks against M4's real endpoint.

**Build order:**

1. **Camera** (`?app=camera`).
   - Starts on opening only, using the front camera, with a switch to the back one.
   - The viewfinder shows the gold HONEST frame (its text comes from settings). The shutter draws the frame into the image.
   - Each shot goes to Your shots, and `notify('photo-taken')` fires.
   - The camera's tracks stop on leaving, going home or hiding the tab.
   - If access is refused or there's no camera, "Choose a photo instead" opens the picker (`capture="user"`).
2. **Save and Share.** Save downloads the photo. Share uses the Web Share API with files, falling back to Save.
3. **The post sheet:**
   - a preview and an optional name or handle;
   - two unticked boxes: the consent text from settings, and 18+;
   - links to the rules and the privacy policy;
   - ALTCHA, with one idempotency key per photo;
   - the photo shrunk to 2048 px JPEG before upload.

   Its states:
   - sending;
   - `202`: "Waiting for approval", the removal link with a copy button, and `notify('wall-sent')`;
   - clear messages for `400`, `413`, `415` and `429`, with retry using the same key.

**Acceptance criteria**

- The camera never starts without a tap. Its tracks end on leaving.
- A refusal falls back to the picker.
- The network log shows no upload unless the visitor posts.
- A double tap posts once, and the consent text sent matches what was displayed.
- **On the real local stack,** a posted photo appears only after an editor approves it. The served file has no EXIF or GPS, and the removal link works.

**Built (7 Nov 2026, Claude):**

- **Code:** `components/apps/camera/`. Photos' viewer saves, shares and posts Your shots through the same sheet. Specs: `e2e/camera.e2e.ts` (a stubbed camera in Chromium and WebKit emulation), plus unit tests for the frame and the post messages.
- **Decisions:**
  - The camera starts from a "Turn on camera" button, even when the app opens from a link or notification. The permission prompt then always follows a tap.
  - The front camera previews mirrored, and the photo matches the preview. The frame is drawn unmirrored.
  - Shots are made as JPEGs of at most 2048 px when taken, so the post sends the shot as it is. A canvas keeps no metadata. Safari's encoder writes a small EXIF block of its own, with no GPS or camera fields, and the back end strips it.
- **For M6 (Astra):** the removal link is `/wall/remove#<id>.<token>`. It 404s until the removal page lands.
- **Real stack check (local, production build, real ALTCHA, M3 and M4):**
  - A pending photo isn't listed, and its file answers 403.
  - After an editor approves it, it's listed, and the served file is WebP with no EXIF.
  - The link's id and token remove it (204), and the file then answers 403.
