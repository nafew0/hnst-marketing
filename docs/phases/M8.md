# M8: Phone QA (owner: Astra, Waves C–E, about 3 days)

> Paste `docs/RULES.md`, then the phone rules block in `docs/phases/PHONE.md` § 3, then this file, as the first message in the coder's session. Before coding, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` § Satus adaptations (§ E5 covers the phone layout) and `docs/phases/PHONE.md`.

**Depends on:** M2–M7. Tests are written as each app lands; the final run happens on the finished build.
**Files:** `e2e/phone-*.e2e.ts` and `docs/qa/phone.md`. Production fixes stay inside Astra's own files; anything else is reported to its owner.

1. **e2e suites:**
   - the shell: lock screen, home, apps, home again, Back, Esc, deep links and no-JS;
   - notifications;
   - each app's main flow;
   - the wall flow on the real local stack;
   - the camera with Chromium's fake media stream, and the refused path.
2. **Browsers:** WebKit and Chromium mobile emulation (iPhone SE, iPhone 15, Pixel 7). At desktop size, `windows.e2e.ts` and the existing suites must stay green.
3. **Accessibility:**
   - axe on every phone state;
   - Lighthouse **accessibility** ≥ 95 on `/` and the app URLs, at mobile size;
   - keyboard and focus, labels and errors;
   - reduced motion and tier 0.
4. **SEO:**
   - metadata, canonical and JSON-LD are unchanged on content URLs;
   - `?app=` views, the wall and the removal page are noindex and out of the sitemap;
   - `robots` is unchanged.
5. **Privacy:**
   - no visitor input in browser storage;
   - analytics only after consent;
   - the camera is off after leaving.
6. **The QA report:** what was tested, the results, and what wasn't tested (real devices aren't used). Cross-owner issues are listed with owner, route, steps and expected result.

**Not tested:** mobile LCP and Lighthouse performance (Nafew, 6 Nov).

**Acceptance criteria**

- Every suite passes in CI.
- Axe finds no serious or critical issues.
- The report is filed.
