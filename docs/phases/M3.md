# M3: CMS for the phone (owner: Astra, Wave B, about 3 days, alongside M2)

> Paste `docs/RULES.md`, then the phone rules block in `docs/phases/PHONE.md` § 3, then this file, as the first message in the coder's session. Before coding, read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` § Satus adaptations (§ E5 covers the phone layout) and `docs/phases/PHONE.md`.

**Depends on:** M1 (settings types and wall contract).

**Build order:**

1. **`wallPhotos` first (day 1), because M4 writes into it.** Build it as an upload collection.
   - **Fields:**
     - the image and its status: pending, approved, rejected or removed;
     - the consent text exactly as sent, the consent time, and `adult`;
     - a `publicId` UUID, which is the contract's `id` (never expose the database id);
     - `displayName`, `removeTokenHash` and `reports`.
   - **Storage:** files go in `env.WALL_MEDIA_DIR`, outside `public/`. A 400 px thumbnail is generated by Payload's sharp.
   - **Access:**
     - The public reads approved photos only, and that applies to the files as well as the records.
     - There's no public create. M4 writes through the Local API.
     - Editors and admins update and delete.
   - **Admin list:** opens filtered to pending, with columns for the thumbnail, name, date and reports.
   - **Hooks:** a status change revalidates the wall's cache tag. Changing a photo to removed deletes its file.
2. **Phone fields in site settings,** matching M1's types: the notifications list, widget copy, camera frame text, wall consent text, wall rules link and the lock-screen switch. Unapproved text gets the existing [DRAFT] marking.
3. **A `phoneNotes` collection** for the Notes app: title, rich-text body and order, with drafts. The public reads published notes only.
4. **Legal drafts:** a "Wall rules" legal document and a wall section in the privacy policy. Both stay drafts until the Founder approves them, and are never published as final.
5. **Seed data** for local development: placeholder copy marked DRAFT, and no real people.
6. **Migrations,** generated and tested on a fresh database. Run `bunx payload migrate` with `</dev/null`.

**Acceptance criteria**

- `bun run check` passes, and migrations apply to a fresh database.
- **Access tests:** anonymous visitors can't read pending records or files. An editor can approve. The public can read approved photos.
- Screenshots of the admin list and the settings fields are attached to the PR.
